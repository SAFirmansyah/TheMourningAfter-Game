# CUTSCENE: Inciting Incident
@text
There’s no shortage of poetry about time.

The foe no one can conquer, the force that flows untouched by any other…
None of that means much to you. You’re only fourteen, after all.

You’ve all the time in the world.

The greatest losses are the ones nobody can warn you of,
So when a stranger arrived in the dead of night to your home and stole from it,
You wake up changed, to find some didn’t wake at all, you break.

No one can name what had happened. A silence like none other falls over Silvaluca.
The stranger, just as unnamed, had taken time itself, touched the untouchable,
And now, it means so much to you.

Elders died where they’d lain.
Adults lost the spryness in their step, the sharpness of their wit.
Years had been shaved off everyone’s lives, unequal in measure.
Children could no longer call themselves children, and you’re one of them.

Now twenty-four, you almost think you got it good.
You’d always wanted to grow up faster. Be taken more seriously,
Be given command and the trust that goes along with it.
But the one who would’ve given you that, the only light you’ve known,
Was snuffed, and would not be restored with dawn…

Your mother is gone.
@end
