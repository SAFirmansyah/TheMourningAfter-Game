# CUTSCENE: The Plague
@text
Behind every surrender is a spirit too broken to fight.
Behind every fight is a soul too desperate to surrender.

Behind every story, lies another.
Your time-thieving foes, the witches that brought Silvaluca to its knees…
Were once stolen from much the same. Not by outsiders,
Not by anybody they could paint up as perfect villains, no—
Rather, by a force of nature they could not cull.

Once upon a time, far, far north, where your people have never ventured,
Flourished a people not too different from your own, give or take a few occult interests…
They were called Burhana.

Then, a plague ravaged through their numbers. A manageable nuisance at first,
Fevers and coughing fits that soon confined them to their beds,
Then led them to their burning pyres.

Mere dozens were left standing – hardly something you’d call a civilization anymore.
So, they moved, hoping not to carry with them the deadly disease.

But an illness far worse did fester amongst those sparse survivors—
An obsession with death itself… an all-consuming desire to overcome it.

“Can one live forever?” was no longer a question to the sisters of Huyana.
It was their only reason to live.
@end
