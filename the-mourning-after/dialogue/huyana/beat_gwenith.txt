# NARRATIVE BEAT: Gwenith
@walk constance Campfire
@walk gwenith FireRight
GWENITH (scared): Sister?
CONSTANCE (neutral): Ah, Gwen, hello–
CONSTANCE (sad): What’s wrong? You look…
GWENITH (scared): I need to tell you something. Something I’ve been doing.
CONSTANCE (neutral): You make it sound like you’ve done something bad. But Gwen, I know you.
CONSTANCE (neutral): You’d never do something you deem bad. So you must just be scared it’s something that I deem bad.
GWENITH (pout): That’s right…
GWENITH (neutral): Our lost sisters. I’ve been into their containments.
CONSTANCE (neutral): Gwenith! That isn’t safe, that’s why nobody goes in but me, you know this–
GWENITH (pout): They’re still our sisters! And I had a good reason!
CONSTANCE (sad): What could possibly warrant risking your life like that? As if we haven’t suffered enough!
CONSTANCE (sad): As if our entire mission isn’t to prevent needless loss of life–
GWENITH (pout): Sister! Listen to me, please.
GWENITH (pout): I wanted to study them. All forms of life fall under my field of study, and the lost sisters are an entirely new life form.
GWENITH (pout): I would be failing my responsibilities to Huyana if I didn’t follow through on the experiments that created the mutations.
CONSTANCE (neutral): Thank your stars you are unscathed. So, what exactly did you study, you madlady?
GWENITH (lost in thought): I took as much as I could from them without hurting them… at least, significantly.
CONSTANCE (neutral): What–
GWENITH (lost in thought): Scales, nails, scabs… teeth, even–
CONSTANCE (neutral): You took out their teeth?!
GWENITH (neutral): They weren’t going to miss one or two, and studying them is too important.
GWENITH (lost in thought): And I’ve understood a great many things about the… whatever they’ve become now.
GWENITH (lost in thought): ‘Lost sisters’ really doesn’t capture what they are. They’re so different from us, Connie.
GWENITH (lost in thought): In more ways than just appearance.
CONSTANCE (sad): What do you mean?
GWENITH (neutral): They’re incredible. Sister, don’t you get it?
GWENITH (lost in thought): Those experiments were meant to make them resistant to death, yes…
GWENITH (lost in thought): But they didn’t fail. Barring the side-effects, they actually worked.
GWENITH (neutral): To some extent, anyway.
CONSTANCE (lost in thought): How do you say?
GWENITH (neutral): Every single lost sister is enhanced in some attribute that could be considered a human’s weakness.
GWENITH (lost in thought): Tougher exteriors, more seamless forms…
GWENITH (scared): And when I studied the stuff within…
GWENITH (neutral): Yes, they hold so much more vitality. What’s cut grows back faster…
GWENITH (neutral): …they preserve themselves better, aging slower…
GWENITH (neutral): …or simply need less to get by.
CONSTANCE (happy): Truly? That’s… that’s blessed, Gwen.
CONSTANCE (neutral): The failures feel so much lighter now.
CONSTANCE (praying): The loss of our sisters’ humanity wasn’t for nothing.
CONSTANCE (happy): In fact…
