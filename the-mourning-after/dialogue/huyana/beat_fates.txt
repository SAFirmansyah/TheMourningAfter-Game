# NARRATIVE BEAT: The Fates
@walk constance Campfire
CONSTANCE (sad): I can’t believe it.
CONSTANCE (sad): This is it.
CONSTANCE (neutral): Our cause is a lost one. We’re done, we’re out. No one left to conduct our research on, no more sisters to lose.
@show archi Entrance
@show seira Entrance
@show kano Entrance
@move archi FireLeft
@move seira FireBack
@move kano FireRight
@settle
ARCHI, SEIRA, KANO (neutral): Constance?
CONSTANCE (neutral): Sisters, hello. What brings you here?
ARCHI (neutral): Well, it’s hardly like we have much to do in the other areas of Huyana.
ARCHI (neutral): We’re at the end of the rope, aren’t we?
CONSTANCE (sad): You’re quite right. For what it’s worth, you’re welcome here as always.
SEIRA (neutral): Say, we had something to bring up with you. A proposition, if you will.
CONSTANCE (neutral): Of course. What is it?
KANO (neutral): Our experiments. We are out of sisters to conduct them on. No blood left to spill. Except for ours, that is.
CONSTANCE (sad): What? But you hadn’t wanted to be a part of that at the start.
CONSTANCE (sad): Sisters, please. You know Huyana would sooner perish than prosper at the cost of unwilling sacrifices.
ARCHI, SEIRA, KANO (neutral): We’ve changed our minds, but we do truly want this. For ourselves as much as for all our sisters.
CONSTANCE (lost in thought): You’re certain?
ARCHI, SEIRA, KANO (neutral): Yes. But let us make this clear: we only want this if it will be our best shot at success.
ARCHI, SEIRA, KANO (neutral): So please, as the most capable among us, we beg of you:
ARCHI, SEIRA, KANO (neutral): Try what you’ve always wondered about trying, but never seen reason to.
ARCHI, SEIRA, KANO (neutral): The most ambitious magic you can conjure, the most dangerous, in fact.
CONSTANCE (sad): Oh…
CONSTANCE (neutral): You’re putting me in such a dire position by giving me the liberty to essentially do anything.
CONSTANCE (sad): Is that how little you value your own lives, now, sisters?
ARCHI, SEIRA, KANO (neutral): It’s the opposite. We value life too much, as a whole, no matter whose.
ARCHI, SEIRA, KANO (neutral): Come on now, sister Constance.
# Fade to black. Open in Huyana's Altar at the altar podium, with Constance, Archi, Seira, Kano, Shuyi, Rudrama, and Gwenith.
@fade black
@room altar Podium
@show archi ArchiSpot
@show seira SeiraSpot
@show kano KanoSpot
@show shuyi ShuyiSpot
@show rudrama RudramaSpot
@show gwenith GwenithSpot
@fade in
CONSTANCE (sad): I can’t fathom this. We’re the last ones left standing.
CONSTANCE (sad): Sisters, are you sure? I have to ask again, before doing anything.
ARCHI, SEIRA, KANO (neutral): Yes. Yes, Constance. Hurry along, now…
SHUYI (stern): Everything I’ve taught you, my girl. Everything you’ve taught yourself, too.
GWENITH (scared): Please, please let this work. I’m praying with all I am.
RUDRAMA (melancholic): If this doesn’t work… oh hell, I’ll miss you three.
CONSTANCE (praying): Thank you, sisters. One last, long shot. Here goes… everything.
# Fade to white. Reopen at the same spot, but without Archi, Seira, and Kano.
@fade white
@hide archi seira kano
@wait 4
@fade in
RUDRAMA (angry): What happened? Where did they go?
GWENITH (scared): I don’t know, I could barely see a thing in here, it got so bright…
SHUYI (sad): Constance?
CONSTANCE (praying): …
CONSTANCE (sad): …
SHUYI (neutral): Constance?
GWENITH (scared): It didn’t work, did it? They’re just… gone.
RUDRAMA (melancholic): Well, drat.
RUDRAMA (angry): Of course it didn’t. Nothing does, not here, not for us.
CONSTANCE (sad): Oh, gods…
GWENITH (pout): They didn’t even turn into other forms like our lost sisters. There’s nothing even to study from this.
SHUYI (sad): Nothing with which to perform last rites, either…
RUDRAMA (angry): I’ve had it. Why do we even try?
# Rudrama, Shuyi, and Gwenith exit the Altar room, but Constance stays behind.
@exit rudrama shuyi gwenith
CONSTANCE (praying): Gods…
CONSTANCE (praying): Please, let something come of this. Let this be what pushes us over the line.
CONSTANCE (praying): Let this… complete the spell.
