# NARRATIVE BEAT: Rudrama
@walk constance Campfire
@walk rudrama FireRight
RUDRAMA (angry): Constance.
CONSTANCE (sad): You look agitated, Rudy. You haven’t not looked that way in a while now, either.
RUDRAMA (angry): Rudy? You’ve not called me that in longer. I don’t know what to think of that.
RUDRAMA (angry): I don’t know what to think of anything anymore.
CONSTANCE (neutral): Do you not want me to call you that?
RUDRAMA (angry): Why do you want to call me that? We didn’t call things off on good terms, ‘Connie’.
RUDRAMA (angry): Circumstances forced us back together here.
RUDRAMA (angry): I thought we were both pretty grudging about that.
CONSTANCE (sad): I know. I thought so, too.
CONSTANCE (sad): But are we, Rudy? Even while everything falls apart around us? Again?
RUDRAMA (neutral): I went out earlier tonight. To scout for the lost sisters that escaped.
RUDRAMA (neutral): The forest turned up rather empty. I made my way to the clearing with that one kingdom in it.
RUDRAMA (neutral): Silvaluca, they call themselves.
CONSTANCE (lost in thought): Our lost sisters made it all the way there?
RUDRAMA (angry): Those folks have fortified their place well enough that none got through. I managed to round them up outside…
RUDRAMA (angry): …but while I was in their town, I heard things. Rumours of sightings.
CONSTANCE (sad): Oh, no.
RUDRAMA (angry): They used such strong, hostile words for our sisters, Connie.
RUDRAMA (angry): Mutated. Deformed. Mangled, cursed, malformed–
RUDRAMA (angry): I couldn’t take it. What do they even know?
CONSTANCE (sad): Oh, Rudy…
CONSTANCE (sad): Did the people there believe those rumours?
RUDRAMA (angry): Of course not. We’re not even real to those people, and who can blame them?
RUDRAMA (angry): Our world’s crumbling. Even I can hardly tell what’s real anymore.
CONSTANCE (sad): I can understand. In that, at least, we’re together.
CONSTANCE (sad): Why don’t we find something real to hold onto together, then?
RUDRAMA (melancholic): That’s a lot to ask of an old flame, Constance.
RUDRAMA (neutral): I’ve trusted you as a leader. That’s trusting you with my life.
RUDRAMA (neutral): But to trust you with my faith, and my heart?
