SHUYI (light-hearted): And you got over three decades of it! That’s an interesting way to call me old.
CONSTANCE (happy): No, no… but I do wish you’d tell us more stories. I want to know everything…

CONSTANCE (lost in thought): Remind me of it, won’t you?
SHUYI (neutral): For what joy?

CONSTANCE (sad): Oh, no. I know Huyana isn’t Burhana, and it never will be.
SHUYI (stern): Think about that for a moment longer. You are right, but why is that so, Constance?
CONSTANCE (neutral): Because some changes are irreversible. What we lost back there…
CONSTANCE (sad): We can’t ever hope to get back.
SHUYI (neutral): Too true.
SHUYI (neutral): But here’s what I want to get through your skull. Consider it another lesson from your old mentor.
CONSTANCE (happy): If only the stakes this time were simply grades on a sheet, hm?
CONSTANCE (neutral): But do tell.
SHUYI (neutral): I want you to know that what we lose in our sanctuary every day is not like what we lost then.
SHUYI (stern): The lost sisters are not like our fallen Burhana kin.
SHUYI (sad): They turned from people into these new, painful forms.
SHUYI (neutral): But they are still with us. Still alive.
CONSTANCE (lost in thought): So, are you saying they could turn back?
SHUYI (neutral): It’s infinitely more possible than the losses that brought us here.
SHUYI (neutral): So your lesson today is to treat them with infinitely more hope.
