# CUTSCENE: Huyana’s Hubris (cast-the-spell route; not played yet)
@text
Behind every fight is a spirit too desperate to surrender.

Some causes are simply too precious to give up. The sisters of Huyana would never know if theirs – a cure to death – was truly one such cause.

But having decided it was, they won the race against time.

The people of Silvaluca bore the cost of the most punishing magic ever fathomed.
A boy of fourteen wakes up twenty-four. Many do not wake at all.
The spell was meant to take days, and instead, it took years. An uneven slice shaven off every citizen, not one spared.

The rumours of strange, mutated creatures outside their walls would be proven true, every last lost sister having been filled with vitality.
They barge through their containment walls and infest the forest between Huyana and Silvaluca, but fall by the blade of a lad who appears to be assisted by fate itself.

The sisters of Huyana fall in battle, leaving Constance at the Altar, on her knees with no one left to fight for.

The cycle of pain and death never would end.
This is the mourning after.
