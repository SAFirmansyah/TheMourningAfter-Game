# CUTSCENE: Back to Burhana
@text
Behind every surrender is a spirit too broken to fight.

Some causes are simply too lost to continue.
The sisters of Huyana would never know if theirs – the cure to death – was truly one such cause.
But having decided it was, they let time win the race.

The people of Silvaluca would never know what they could’ve lost that night.
A boy of fourteen has a troubled night, dreaming of a stranger that walked their roads, and took something precious from his people.
But he wakes the next morning, still fourteen, and goes about his day.

The rumours of strange, mutated creatures outside their walls would be proven true, but not pose much of a threat to the combined might of the Silvalu forces.

The sisters of Huyana would pass to the next world and meet again their lost sisters, and everybody the plague had taken from Burhana, too.

The cycle of pain and death never would begin.
There would be no mourning after.
@end
