# The Mourning After – Chronological Narrative

From the ruins of a plague-ridden civilization known as Burhana, a group of survivors establish their place in a forest, and settle off the outskirts of Silvaluca, a large Kingdom where people are pragmatic and militarized and painfully 'normal'.

The survivors are women. They practice magic. In mourning, they grow closer, and develop an obsession with overcoming the source of their mourning – they wish for a cure to death itself.

The obsession grows into something cult-like, headed by Constance, a middle-aged scholarly matriarch who leads with a spirit of pushing the limits of human nature through magic, and self-sacrifice but only through will. She is motivated by the loss of her only child to the plague.

Many members of this Cult, now known as Huyana, volunteer themselves to be the subjects of Constance's experimental spells, in hopes that they'll contribute to the knowledge that will eventually culminate in a cure for death. They all believe their cause is noble, that it'll benefit all of humanity.

But death really isn't easy to cure. The experiments fail, and bear repercussions for the members of Huyana. They become deformed like zombies at best, or mutate into monstrous creatures with unique anatomies and dangerous powers. Despite this, the others in Huyana recognize them as their own people, and vow to keep them safe and well-tended in a Sanctuary of sorts.

Constance's dear younger sister, Gwenith, takes on the responsibility of growing the various magical crops needed to feed the mutants, along with the ingredients Shuyi needs for medicines to cure the ones that fall sick. She's deeply philosophical, especially for her age.

Shuyi, an elderly woman who had once been Constance's mentor and confidante, is now her only solace. She's obsessed with saving lives, and is wary of the experiments that turn their members into a shell of their true selves, seeing this as a fate no better than death. She's a rationalist, after all. Her responsibility is to cure sick members by mixing up medicines for their specific anatomies.

Then, there's Rudrama, who had once upon a time perhaps been with Constance in ways that transcend friendship, but it's been too long since then. She's a simple-minded fighter, interested in forging the chains and cages that secure the mutants, and keeping them strong so nobody is lost to a breach of containment.

Gwenith conducts research on the mutants, taking samples of their skin, scales, horns, etc., under the belief that the members who had once pledged to potentially lethal experiments wouldn't object to this research. She shares her findings that there are unique properties to the mutants: they heal faster than people do, or they're stronger, or more resistant to injury. Hearing this, Constance gains insight into the positive fruits of her experiments. Part of a spell is revealed to her.

As resources dwindle and more and more members turn into mutants, it becomes harder to keep every member (mutant or otherwise) fed and taken care of. Still-human members go out to the forest to gather resources, but their numbers go down as the number of mutants that need those resources increases.

Shuyi approaches Constance and complains about Gwenith's research, stating it's inhumane and takes more of their increasingly valuable resources to heal the mutants being researched on. She believes they aren't human anymore, but hypothesizes that if they could turn from human to mutant, the reverse cannot be ruled out of the realm of possibility. This insight reveals a second part to the spell.

Things grow more dire in Huyana as they officially reach resource shortages so bad that some mutants start to starve, breach containment, and spread illness.

Constance, in a state of distress, confides in her old flame, Rudrama, about her worries. Rudrama insists they use more extreme measures to fix things. She mentions that on one expedition out to Silvaluca to get resources, she heard rumours among the citizens, discussing fantastical creatures – the mutants had been spotted, and suspicions are growing, though most remain disbelieving of such 'tall tales'. A third part to the spell is revealed.

The last human members of Huyana are now starting to accept that their cause is a lost one. None of the experiments work, and progress stalls as resources dry up. Still, they believe with all their hearts that they ought to try until they cannot anymore.

As such, the last three human members approach Constance. Archi, Seira, and Kano – sisters who can't bear the desperation anymore, and want to be part of something big. They urge Constance to go all in, try something ridiculous and ambitious and really make it count. They pledge themselves to the riskiest experiment thus far.

Constance faces resistance from Shuyi, but overrides it and carries out the experiment. The sisters discorporate on the spot, turning to dust and fading away before their eyes. This result puzzles everybody. Gwenith complains of not even having a body left to study. Rudrama simply loses hope.

But in reality, the three sisters become the Fates, able to manipulate the threads of destiny, life, and death, to the fullest extent. It is the result Huyana would've hoped for, except it's backfired. The cost of curing death is no longer being anything one could describe as alive – they have no bodies, no way to communicate, and are completely on their own.

Constance has the fourth and final epiphany after this experiment. The spell is complete. She heads to the Altar, desperate and determined, and writes down a spell to steal from the lifetime of one to supplement the lifetime of another.

Gwenith flips a switch. She thinks it's too far. Shuyi warns Constance of the possibility this goes out of control and wreaks havoc on Silvaluca. Rudrama, on the other hand, sees this as the right move, their one true solution to keep everybody alive and continue their mission.

Constance heeds Rudrama, and casts the spell, knowing full well it's likelier to fail than succeed. The plan's simple: go to the center of Silvaluca and try to take a couple of days off the lifespan of every citizen, and use it to let the much smaller population of Huyana live for more years, and teleport back before anyone realizes.

It's a runaway chain reaction, and entirely chaotic. The Silvalu people lose anywhere between a couple years and a couple decades. Elders die where they stand, adults become incapacitated by rapid onset of age, and kids wake up having missed out on entire childhoods.

Back at the Sanctuary, upon receiving an excess of lifeblood, the mutants and deformed humans break free of their containment, feeling more powerful and full of vitality than ever. Gwenith, Shuyi, and Rudrama accompany the strongest of these beasts, unable to stop them but attempting to protect them, as they rampage out of Huyana and into the forest between Huyana and Silvaluca.

Constance desperately summons herself back to the Sanctuary to realize Huyana's in shambles. But not before being spotted by a certain kid who grew up too fast, Perrin.

Meanwhile, the Silvalu struggle to reckon with the fallout and mourn their dead. Perrin, who was fourteen before getting catapulted to the age of twenty-four, is descended from two military generals of Silvaluca. His father had fallen in an old war, and his mother had taken his place, only to now have had decades stolen off her life and become unable to fight. She appoints her prodigal son the next general, and fills his impressionable head with fantasies of revenge and resolution.

As he prepares to fight, he senses the presence of a spirit over his shoulder. Suspecting divine intervention, he prays for better luck, and gets what he's prayed for. Unbeknownst to him, it's the first of the Fates, changing his lifeblood to grant him better health for the fight ahead. Armed with a sword, he sets off from his base at the border to fight his way through the forest, protecting Silvaluca's walls from deformed humans and lower-level mutants. The resourceful kid uses the parts of these enemies to grow stronger along the way.

Eventually, he reaches the edge of Huyana, and finds himself contending with a much stronger mutant, herded by Rudrama. He's already a bit hurt, and doesn't believe he can take her on. He senses a second Fate, and prays, and finds himself faced suddenly with a different mutant, herded by Shuyi instead. He mistakes the elderly woman for an easy opponent and charges, only to realize she can heal herself in battle, and he falls.

However, just as he's dying, a third powerful sensation strikes him as the third Fate reverses his death. He wakes back at his base, having lost everything he'd gained, as if none of that had ever happened – besides the tokens he'd used to pray to the Fates.

And so he tries again, and again, and again. He throws himself at the enemies, fells mutants of the highest order, and gets further each time. He even makes it into Huyana, seeing how they lived, but falls short of reaching the Sanctuary, where he believes Huyana's leader, the one he'd seen, resides.

Eventually, armed with better luck and all his knowledge that cost him many lives, he makes it there. In the Sanctuary, he faces Constance, as the Fates had desired, and threatens her. "Give back the time you stole," he says, not understanding why it was stolen, or that the ones who had inherited that time have since been slain by his own hand. Nothing is possible now, if it ever was.

Constance hangs her head low in shame and regret. She doesn't fight him like he'd expected, and maybe hoped. He doesn't get his show of honour. She gives up, not seeing any reason to go on… or does she?
